==============================================================
   EEE MINI PROJECT PORTFOLIO  -  STEP BY STEP GUIDE
   (Very easy English)
==============================================================

WHAT IS IT?
-----------
It is a website.
Students write their project work in it.
Teachers check the work and give marks.
An AI helper (the "AI Mentor") asks students questions.


==============================================================
 PART 1 - HOW TO OPEN THE WEBSITE  (everyone)
==============================================================

Step 1. Open the website link your teacher gave you.
Step 2. Log in with your college email (....@kletech.ac.in).
Step 3. If it asks for a code:
          - Type your email.
          - Click "Send sign-in code".
          - Open your email. You will see a 6-number code.
          - Type the code and click "Verify & sign in".
Step 4. You are inside!

   If it says "Access not yet granted":
   your email is not added. Tell the admin.


==============================================================
 PART 2 - FOR STUDENTS
==============================================================

--- A. Fill your project work ---

Step 1. Click "My Portfolio" on the left side.
Step 2. You will see the steps of your project:
           Project Profile, S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, Debug Log, S7, Viva
Step 3. Click "Project Profile" first.
          - Write your project title.
          - Write your project website link (if you have one).
Step 4. Click "S1". Fill the boxes and the table.
Step 5. Click the blue "Save" button. ALWAYS SAVE!
Step 6. Do the same for the next steps, one by one.

   REMEMBER:
   * You CANNOT copy or paste. Type everything yourself.
   * Some pages have a "Sign & date" button.
     After you sign, that page gets LOCKED.
   * S6 and S7 open only after your teacher passes Review 3.


--- B. Upload photos and files ---

Step 1. Find the box that says "Click to upload".
Step 2. Click it and choose your photo or file.
        (Or click "Add link" to add a Drive or GitHub link.)
Step 3. Wait for "Uploaded".


--- C. See your teacher's feedback ---

Step 1. Click "Reviews & Feedback" on the left side.
Step 2. Read the comments from your teacher
        for R1, R2, R3, R4 and Final.


--- D. Take the AI quiz (30 seconds per question) ---

Step 1. First SAVE your work in a stage (for example S1).
Step 2. Click "AI Mentor" on the left side.
Step 3. Choose the stage (for example "S1").
Step 4. Click "Start quiz on my S1 entries".
Step 5. The AI asks a question about what YOUR TEAM wrote.
Step 6. You have 30 SECONDS. Type your answer.
        Press Enter (or click "Submit answer").
        If time finishes, your answer is sent by itself.
Step 7. The AI tells you:
          - Right   -> "Correct" and why.
          - Wrong   -> "You answered ... but ..."
                       and "The correct way to answer is ..."
Step 8. Click "Next question". Do all 5 questions.
Step 9. You get a score out of 10.

        9 or 10 = Excellent
        7 or 8  = Good
        4 to 6  = Need more work
        0 to 3  = Need a lot of work

   (Your teacher can see your answers and your score.)


--- E. Use your own free AI key (only if needed) ---

Step 1. Go to  aistudio.google.com/apikey
Step 2. Click "Create API key". Copy it.
Step 3. In AI Mentor, open "My Gemini key".
Step 4. Paste the key. Click "Save & test".
   Never share your key with anyone.


==============================================================
 PART 3 - FOR TEACHERS (Guides)
==============================================================

Step 1. Log in. Click "My Teams".
Step 2. Click a team name to open its work.
Step 3. Read each stage. Write comments under any box
        (click "Comment").
Step 4. Click the "Reviews & Assessment" tab.
Step 5. For the review (R1, R2, R3, R4 or Final):
          - Tick the boxes that are done.
          - Choose a grade.
          - Type marks.
          - Choose "Cleared" or "Not cleared".
          - Write feedback for the students.
          - Click "Save review".
Step 6. To see the AI quiz answers, click the "AI Mentor" tab.

   Review 3 is a BIG GATE.
   Students can go to S6 only after you choose "Cleared".


==============================================================
 PART 4 - FOR ADMIN / HoD / CO-ORDINATOR / REVIEWER
==============================================================

Step 1. Click "Administration" on the left side.
Step 2. Students tab -> click
        "Import 2026-27 class list & email IDs"
        (this adds all teams, students and emails in one click).
Step 3. Faculty tab -> add or edit teachers.
        Choose the role: Faculty, Coordinator, Reviewer or HoD/Admin.
Step 4. Teams tab -> edit team title, theme and guide.
Step 5. To remind students, click the blue "Send reminder" button.
        Choose the teams, write the message, click "Send reminder".
Step 6. (HoD/Admin only) Gemini AI tab -> click "Test"
        to check that the AI works.


==============================================================
 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
==============================================================

 Problem                        What to do
 ----------------------------   ----------------------------------
 Cannot log in                  Ask admin to add your email.
 Did not get the code           Check the Spam folder. Try again.
 "Pasting is not allowed"       That is correct. Type it yourself.
 Cannot edit S6 or S7           Teacher must clear Review 3 first.
 Quiz does not start            Save something in that stage first.
 AI does not answer             Try again, or add your own AI key.
 Page is locked                 Ask your teacher to "Unlock" it.
