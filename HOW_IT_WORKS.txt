   EEE MINI PROJECT PORTFOLIO  -  HOW IT WORKS (EASY GUIDE)
===========================================================

WHAT IS THIS?
-------------
This is a website for our EEE department.
Students keep all their mini project work here.
Teachers check the work and give marks here.
It is like a big online notebook for every project team.


WHO USES IT?  (4 KINDS OF PEOPLE)
--------------------------------
1. ADMIN (HoD / Boss)
   - Can see EVERYTHING.
   - Adds teachers, adds students, makes teams.
   - Puts in the department AI key and changes the settings.
   - Admin emails: the owner of the Google account
     AND shreyashalingali@gmail.com

2. CO-ORDINATOR and REVIEWER
   - Can open "Administration": add teachers, students, teams,
     send reminders and read the audit log.
   - Can see EVERY team's portfolio and give reviews.
   - Cannot see the AI keys or change the settings.
   - Cannot make anyone an Admin.
   (Admin sets this: Administration -> Faculty -> Edit -> Role)

3. TEACHER (Guide)
   - Sees only HIS or HER own teams.
   - Reads the students' work.
   - Writes comments and gives marks.
   - Cannot change the students' work.

4. STUDENT
   - Sees only HIS or HER own team.
   - Fills tables, writes answers, uploads photos and files.
   - Adds the project website link in "Project Profile".
   - Sees all review feedback (R1, R2 ...) in "Reviews & Feedback"
     and also at the top of each stage page.
   - Can talk to the AI Mentor, and can use his or her OWN free
     Gemini key (AI Mentor -> "My Gemini key").
   - AI Mentor "Timed quiz on my saved work":
       1. First fill in and SAVE the stage (for example S1).
       2. Choose the stage and press "Start quiz". The AI reads what
          YOUR TEAM WROTE and asks 5 questions about it, one by one.
          (Example: "Your FR2 says 50 mV - why that number?")
       3. You get 30 SECONDS for each question. When time is up,
          your answer is sent by itself.
       4. After EVERY answer the mentor talks back:
             correct   -> it says why it is right
             wrong     -> "You answered ... but ..." and then
                          "The correct way to answer is ..."
       5. Each answer gets 0, 1 or 2 points -> score out of 10
          (9-10 Exemplary, 7-8 Competent, 4-6 Developing, 0-3 Inadequate).
       6. Teachers can read every question, answer, time and mark.
     "Score my saved work /10" checks your saved worksheets against
     the handbook. Both are advice only - teachers give the real marks.
   - CANNOT COPY OR PASTE in the worksheets (computer AND mobile). Everything must be typed
     in your own words. (Pasting is allowed only for links and
     for the AI key.)


WHERE IS EVERYTHING KEPT?
-------------------------
- Google Sheet  = the "memory". All writing is saved here.
- Google Drive  = the "cupboard". All photos and files go here.
- Gemini AI     = the "helper teacher" (AI Mentor).
- The website   = the "door". Everyone enters through it.

You do NOT need to open the Google Sheet. The website does
everything by itself.


THE PROJECT STEPS (LIKE CLIMBING STAIRS)
----------------------------------------
Every team climbs these steps one by one. No skipping!

  Profile  ->  Who is in the team and what are we making?
  S1       ->  Write what the project MUST do, with numbers.
               (Example: "Output must stay within 1%")
  S2       ->  Draw the block diagram (the picture of the system).
  S3       ->  MEASURE the real parts in the lab.
  S4       ->  Do the calculations using those measurements.
  S5-PRE   ->  GUESS the results BEFORE switching on.
               Everyone signs it. After signing, the guess
               cannot be changed. (No cheating!)
  S5-BUILD ->  Build it and test it.
  S6       ->  Make the control loop work.
               (This opens ONLY after the teacher passes Review 3.)
  Debug Log->  Write every problem you faced and how you fixed it.
  S7       ->  Check every number from S1. Pass or fail?
  S7-VIVA  ->  Each student writes alone what he or she learned.


SIGNING AND LOCKING
-------------------
Some pages have a "Sign & date" button.
When you sign, the page gets LOCKED with the date and time.
After that you can only fill the "measured result" boxes.
Only a teacher can unlock it, and the teacher must write why.


REVIEWS AND MARKS
-----------------
There are 5 reviews: R1, R2, R3, R4 and Final.
The teacher ticks boxes, picks a grade and gives marks.
Review 3 is a BIG GATE. If the team does not pass it,
they cannot go to step S6.
Total marks = 100.


THE AI MENTOR
-------------
It is a robot helper inside the website.
It asks the students questions to make them THINK.
It will NOT do the homework for them.
Teachers can read every chat and add notes.


HOW TO START (FOR THE ADMIN)
----------------------------
1. Open the website link.
2. Go to "Administration".
3. Add teachers   (Faculty tab).
4. Make teams     (Teams tab) - pick theme and guide.
5. Students tab  - press "Import 2026-27 class list" to add
                    all 17 teams and 54 students (by SRN) at once.
                    Then add each person's email so they can log in.
6. Gemini AI tab  - press "Test" to check the AI key works.
Done! Now students and teachers can log in.


HOW DO PEOPLE LOG IN?
--------------------
- With a college Google account: it opens by itself.
- With normal Gmail: type your email, get a 6-number code
  in your email, type the code, and you are in.
- If your email is not added by the admin, you cannot enter.


IS IT SAFE?
-----------
- The secret AI key is hidden on Google's server.
  It is NEVER sent to anyone's phone or computer.
- The website checks WHO you are before every action.
  A student cannot see another team's work.
- Right click, copy and "inspect" are blocked on the page.
- Every change is written in a diary called "Audit log".


IT WORKS ON
-----------
Laptop, computer, tablet and mobile phone.
It also has a dark mode (moon button at the top).


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- Page is blank       -> check the file names: Index, App, Code, Handbook.
- AI does not answer  -> Admin goes to Gemini AI tab and presses "Test".
- Cannot log in       -> Ask the admin to add your email.
- Changed the code?   -> Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit >
                         New version > Deploy.
