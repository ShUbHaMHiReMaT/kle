==============================================================
   HOW TO GET YOUR OWN FREE GEMINI API KEY
   (and put it in the AI Mentor)
   Very easy English - step by step
==============================================================

WHAT IS AN API KEY?
-------------------
An API key is like a secret password for the AI.
With your own key, the AI Mentor uses YOUR free AI quota.
It is FREE. You only need a Google account.

!! KEEP IT SECRET !!
Do NOT send your key on WhatsApp, email or to friends.
Do NOT paste it in any chat. Only paste it in the AI Mentor box.


==============================================================
 PART 1 - MAKE THE KEY (in Google AI Studio)
==============================================================

Step 1. Open Google Chrome (on a computer or phone).

Step 2. Go to this website:
            aistudio.google.com/apikey

Step 3. Log in with your Google account
        (your college email or your Gmail is fine).

Step 4. The first time, a box may ask you to agree to the terms.
        Read it, tick the box and click "Continue" / "Accept".

Step 5. Click the blue button  "Create API key".

Step 6. If it asks you to choose a project:
          - Choose any project shown, OR
          - Click "Create API key in new project".

Step 7. Wait 2-3 seconds. Your key appears.
        It is a long line of letters and numbers.
        Example (not real):  AIzaSyA1b2C3d4E5f6G7h8I9j0K...
        Some new keys start with  AQ.  - that is also OK.

Step 8. Click the "Copy" button next to the key.
        Now the key is copied.

   TIP: If you close the page, you can always come back to
        aistudio.google.com/apikey and copy the key again.


==============================================================
 PART 2 - PUT THE KEY IN THE AI MENTOR
==============================================================

Step 1. Open the Mini Project Portfolio website and log in.

Step 2. Click "AI Mentor" on the left side.

Step 3. Find the box "My Gemini key".
        Click "Add my own key (optional)".

Step 4. Paste your key in the box:
          - Computer: click in the box, press  Ctrl + V
          - Phone:    press and hold in the box, tap "Paste"
          - Or click the small "Paste" button next to the box.
        (Pasting works ONLY in this box. Everywhere else
         in the portfolio, pasting is blocked.)

Step 5. Leave the "Model" box empty (or type  gemini-2.5-flash ).

Step 6. Click  "Save & test".

Step 7. If you see  "Your key works and is saved securely"
        -> DONE! The badge shows  "Using my key ****1234".


==============================================================
 PART 3 - IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
==============================================================

 Message                              What to do
 -----------------------------------  -----------------------------
 "does not look like a valid key"     You copied only part of it,
                                      or added a space. Copy again.
 "API key not valid"                  Wrong key. Go to AI Studio,
                                      copy the key again.
 "Permission denied" /                Make the key again in AI Studio
 "API not enabled"                    (Step 5 of Part 1).
 "quota" / "too many requests"        Your free limit for today is
                                      used up. Try again tomorrow.
 "Model not found"                    Type  gemini-2.5-flash  in the
                                      Model box and save again.
 Paste does not work                  Use the "Paste" button, or
                                      long-press the box and tap Paste.


==============================================================
 PART 4 - SAFETY RULES
==============================================================

 * Your key is checked, then stored safely on the college server.
 * Nobody (not your team, not teachers) can see your key.
 * The website shows only the last 4 letters.
 * To stop using it: AI Mentor -> "My Gemini key" ->
   "Remove my key".
 * If you think someone saw your key: go to AI Studio,
   DELETE that key, and make a new one.
